About 2BAC-PC-11 Rating

✨ Our Purpose & Spirit

2BAC-PC-11 Rating is built as a shared memory album and entertainment space for the 2BAC PC-11 class. It celebrates student creativity, photography skills, fun editing, science lab memories, and artistic presentation.

⚖️ The Golden Ethics Rule

"Rate the creativity, quality, editing, and presentation of the images — never the physical appearance of people."

Harassment, bullying, or rating personal looks is strictly prohibited and leads to immediate suspension.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 0.1 – 10.0 rating work?

Each guest gets 1 active vote per photo. You can edit your vote anytime. The community average is recalculated atomically.

How does the Recommended feed rank photos?

It uses a virality score combining total votes, discussions/comments, reactions, average rating, and a 24-hour recency boost.

Where are the photos stored?

No files are uploaded to Firebase Storage. Only external image URLs (via postimages.org or imgto.link) are saved.
