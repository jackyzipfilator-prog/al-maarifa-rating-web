About 2BAC-PC-11 Rating

✨ OUR CONCEPT & VIBE

2BAC-PC-11 Rating is a playful, casual rating space created for our 2BAC PC-11 class. We rate girls (and sometimes boys) on looks, visual style, outfit, and personality vibe.

⚖️ THE GOLDEN RULE

“This is only for fun and goofing around between us. It is not serious and the site will be deleted after a period of time. Be respectful. Do not take it too seriously.”

Harassment or bullying is strictly prohibited. Keep it fun and friendly!

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can I change my rating on a photo?

Yes! Click on any score pill or type a new decimal score (0.1 - 10.0) in the photo popup and tap "Update Vote".

Can I change my nickname or profile picture?

Yes! Click on your profile in the top-right navbar or on your comments to edit your nickname, avatar URL, or bio anytime.

Are photos cropped?

No. In the photo popup, images appear in their real aspect ratio (9:16, portrait, or landscape). Click on the photo to open full-screen lightbox!
